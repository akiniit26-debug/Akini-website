COSORI vs Ninja vs Philips: which air fryer fits your kitchen?

These three models solve different kitchen needs. The COSORI CP158-AF is a single-basket 5.5-litre option; Ninja DZ400 and Philips NA350/00 are larger dual-basket appliances.

How this comparison was made: this is a desk-based comparison of manufacturer specifications and product documentation, not a hands-on test. We have not independently tested cooking results, noise, durability or energy use. Specifications can vary by regional version; check the exact model on the retailer's listing before buying.

COSORI CP158-AF: a simpler single-basket layout

The 5.5-litre basket and 1,700 W rated power make this the smallest-capacity option in this comparison. The manual lists a 75–205°C temperature range and a timer from 1 to 60 minutes. COSORI's documentation describes a serving range of roughly three to five people, but actual portions depend on the food and how much space it needs.

Consider it if: you usually cook one food at a time, want a straightforward single drawer, and do not need two separate cooking zones.

Ninja DZ400: the largest stated capacity

Ninja lists 9.5 litres split across two independent drawers. Its SYNC function is designed to finish different foods together, while MATCH copies settings when you want to prepare a larger quantity. The official product page lists six programmes: Air Fry, Max Crisp, Roast, Bake, Dehydrate and Reheat. It is also the heaviest of these three models in the specifications checked: 12.3 kg.

Consider it if: cooking a main and a side separately, or serving a larger household, matters more than keeping the appliance light and compact.

View Ninja on Amazon.it

Philips NA350/00: a 9-litre dual-basket alternative

Philips lists a 9-litre capacity, 2,750 W power, eight presets and a digital touch-screen interface. The product specifications list an appliance weight of 7.85 kg and dishwasher-safe removable parts. Its capacity is slightly below the Ninja DZ400 on paper, while the listed weight is lower; compare the available counter space and basket arrangement before deciding.

Consider it if: you want two baskets and preset programmes, but prefer to compare a different size-and-weight balance against the Ninja.

What to check before you buy

  • Measure the counter: compare the appliance dimensions and leave room around the air outlet as the manual requires.
  • Think about basket layout: one large drawer is not interchangeable with two separate drawers.
  • Check the exact version: model suffixes, included accessories and specifications can differ by country.
  • Check cleaning instructions: confirm which removable parts are dishwasher-safe for the exact model.
  • Check the live listing: prices, stock, delivery and retailer terms can change at any time.

Our practical takeaway

There is no universal winner from specifications alone. Choose the COSORI if a single 5.5-litre drawer matches your cooking routine. Compare the Ninja and Philips if two cooking zones are important; the Ninja offers the larger stated capacity and SYNC/MATCH controls, while Philips lists eight presets and a lower appliance weight in the specifications reviewed.

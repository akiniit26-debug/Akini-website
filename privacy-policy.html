Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This notice explains how AKINI handles personal data when you visit akini.eu, use the contact form or contact the site operator.

1. Data controller and contact

Controller: Mansur Ahmed, operator of AKINI. Privacy contact: akiniit26@gmail.com. Please use this address for requests about your personal data. No Data Protection Officer has been appointed for this website.

2. What data is processed and why

  • Server and security data: when pages are requested, the hosting provider may process IP address, request time, browser/device information and server logs to deliver, maintain and secure the site. The proposed basis is legitimate interests in operating and protecting the website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Contact form and email enquiries: if you submit the contact form, the name, email address, optional phone number and message fields you provide are processed so AKINI can read and respond to your enquiry. The form is supplied by HubSpot and is loaded only after you select the load-form button. The proposed basis is legitimate interests in handling voluntary enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); where a request concerns steps before a contract, Article 6(1)(b) may apply as appropriate. If you email AKINI, your sender address and message are processed for the same purpose.
  • Wishlist and cart shortlist: product names are stored locally in your browser so that the shortlist works. In the current version these lists are not sent to AKINI and are not used to identify you.

Providing email information is voluntary. Without it, AKINI may not be able to reply. Please do not send special-category or other sensitive personal data.

3. Recipients and international transfers

GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process technical request data under its own terms. HubSpot provides the embedded contact form and processes the information you submit through it under its own terms and privacy notice. The email provider you use processes messages you send. Google Analytics 4 is provided by Google and is loaded only after you accept optional analytics in the cookie banner on the English or Italian homepage. The current implementation disables Google Signals and advertising-personalisation signals. Google Signals is not currently activated by AKINI. If AKINI decides to activate it, Google may associate session data with information from signed-in Google users who have enabled Ads Personalization, to provide aggregated reporting such as demographics and interests. AKINI will first update this notice, assess the applicable legal basis and user-rights requirements, and provide any additional consent choices required before activation. Google may process information under its applicable privacy terms. Images and other external resources may be requested from their respective providers, who may receive technical connection data such as your IP address. Their own privacy notices apply. Providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; where personal data is transferred internationally, the relevant provider is responsible for identifying and implementing an applicable transfer mechanism and safeguards. AKINI does not sell personal data.

4. Retention

Enquiries received by email or through HubSpot are kept only as long as needed to answer and manage follow-up. AKINI aims to delete enquiry records within 12 months after the last exchange, unless a longer period is necessary for a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. HubSpot may retain submitted data in its systems under its applicable service terms; contact AKINI to request deletion from records under our control. Hosting logs are retained according to the hosting provider's own retention and security practices. Wishlist and cart data remain in your browser until you clear the relevant site data or remove the items.

5. Your GDPR rights

Subject to legal conditions and exceptions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. To exercise a right, email akiniit26@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or another competent supervisory authority.

6. Cookies, local storage and tracking

The cart and wishlist use browser local storage, which is different from cookies. Google Analytics 4 is activated only after you accept analytics in the cookie banner on the English or Italian homepage; if you reject, the Analytics tag is not loaded. You can change your choice using the Cookie settings control. After consent, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies to measure visits and usage. The current implementation does not enable Google Signals or advertising personalisation. Google Signals may involve associating session data with information from signed-in Google users who have enabled Ads Personalization, including aggregated demographics and interests reporting; AKINI will not activate this feature until the privacy notice and consent controls have been updated and the applicable requirements assessed. AKINI does not currently activate behavioural advertising or Google Signals. After you accept optional Google Analytics, the website sends an affiliate_click event to Google Analytics when you click an Amazon affiliate link, to measure outbound link clicks. This event measures clicks only; it does not track purchases or attribute sales or commissions. See our Cookie Policy.

7. External links and future changes

When you follow a link to a retailer or another third-party website, that provider's privacy notice and practices apply. AKINI does not control their processing. If AKINI changes its analytics, newsletter, advertising or affiliate tracking practices, this notice will be updated to describe the relevant purposes, legal basis, recipients, retention and consent requirements.

8. Automated decisions

AKINI does not currently carry out automated decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects, or profiling of visitors.

9. Updates

This notice describes the services currently enabled on the site and will be revised if those services or the applicable processing change. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

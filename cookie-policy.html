Cookie Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy describes cookies and similar technologies used by the current AKINI website.

Cookies and similar technologies currently used

The cart shortlist and wishlist use browser local storage to remember product names on your device. Local storage is not technically a cookie and can be cleared through your browser's site-data settings. Google Analytics 4 is used to measure visits and general website usage. The Google Analytics tag is not loaded unless you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner. If you reject optional cookies, Google Analytics is not activated. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings control. After consent, Google Analytics may set analytics cookies, such as _ga. The optional HubSpot contact form is not loaded automatically; if you choose to load it, your browser connects to HubSpot, which may set cookies or similar technologies under its own practices.

Hosting and external resources

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. External images or resources may be loaded from third-party providers, which can receive technical connection data such as your IP address. This policy does not claim that third-party services never use cookies; consult the relevant provider's own notice and browser controls.

Affiliate and advertising tracking

AKINI does not currently activate behavioural advertising or affiliate-attribution technologies. Google Analytics is an optional analytics service and is loaded only after you accept it in the cookie banner on the English or Italian homepage. The HubSpot form is activated only after you select the explicit load-form button on the Contact page. If you do not wish to connect to that third-party service, do not select that button and contact AKINI by email.

Managing browser data

You can manage or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing local storage may remove your cart shortlist and wishlist. Blocking all storage may affect some site functions.
